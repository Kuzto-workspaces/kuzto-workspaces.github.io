Our Story

Born from passion. Built with precision.

Kuzto didn't start as a business. It started at a desk, late at night, putting together a gaming rig for a friend. Then a trading terminal for another. Then a creator's editing bay. Word spread.

Every project was a close collaboration. We'd figure out exactly what someone needed, find the right parts, build the custom woodwork, and obsess over every cable until it disappeared. No marketing. No overhead. Just the work.

Now we're making it real. Same care, same transparency, same obsession with getting the environment right.
